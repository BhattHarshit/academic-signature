# Notices and acknowledgements

Academic Signature is an MIT-licensed academic website starter built on the al-folio/Jekyll ecosystem. Its local presentation, profile configuration, full-site search, CV modes, sitemap, documentation, and supporting interface code are maintained as part of Academic Signature.

## al-folio

Academic Signature derives from [al-folio](https://github.com/alshedivat/al-folio), originally authored by Maruan Al-Shedivat and maintained by the al-folio community. al-folio is distributed under the MIT License. Its copyright notice is retained in `LICENSE`.

Font Awesome and Academicons are loaded as external libraries rather than redistributed as bundled font packages. Other Ruby, Python, browser-side, and workflow dependencies are declared in `Gemfile`, `_academic_signature/requirements.txt`, `_config.yml`, and `.github/workflows/`; those projects retain their own copyright and license terms.

The MIT licenses used by al-folio and Jekyll do not require a visible credit in the generated website footer. Academic Signature therefore keeps the public footer concise while preserving software provenance in this repository.
