## Overview

This course develops the mathematical and computational foundations of numerical linear algebra, with attention to stability, conditioning, efficient implementation, and the structure of large-scale problems.

## Topics

- Orthogonal factorizations and least-squares problems
- Eigenvalue and singular-value computations
- Conditioning, stability, and backward error
- Iterative and randomized methods for large problems
